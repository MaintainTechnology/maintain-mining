# Maintain Mining — Product Truth

Durable facts about what this repo represents. Visual decisions live in
[DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md); this file records the truths design must not contradict.

## What this is

The brand and design-system repository for **Maintain Mining**, the Maintain
group's mining-sector brand: predictive maintenance and asset intelligence for
mining operations — the fleet, the plant and the pit. The repo's product *is*
the brand kit: tokens, logo, icons, imagery, component patterns, and the living
style guide (`design-system/index.html`).

## Audience

- Designers and marketers producing Maintain Mining visuals (decks, social,
  reports, letterhead, web).
- Engineers and AI agents building Maintain Mining web/product surfaces from
  the tokens.

## Brand commitments (pinned)

- **Graphite `#303234` (48, 50, 52) is the brand colour** (user-pinned,
  supersedes the earlier red-purple direction): the grayish black of the
  heavy-machinery world (Komatsu / Caterpillar register). It is the logo
  colour on light surfaces; the logo inverts to white on dark grounds.
- **Safety amber `#FFB800`** is the single working accent — primary actions,
  hazard highlights, keylines — always with pit-black ink, one amber element
  per surface.
- The **Maintain family structural system** is inherited: Albert Sans + Inter,
  the 4px spacing scale, survey-grid texture, wireframe terrain imagery
  (now desaturated to charcoal).
- **Status severity colours are functional, not brand** — never rebranded.
- The wordmark's **MINING sub-word is drawn geometry**, never font text.

## Constraints

- Static repo: no build step, no npm dependencies. Python 3 + Pillow only
  (graphics pipeline).
- No invented commercial facts: no domains, legal entities, ABNs, prices, or
  customer names anywhere. Open items are tracked in DESIGN.md §10.
- Demonstration content in the style guide is specimen data and is labeled as
  such.
