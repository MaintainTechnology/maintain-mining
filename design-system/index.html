Maintain Mining Design System

Tokens, assets and patterns — one source of truth.

The reference for every Maintain Mining visual: social graphics, branded reports, letterhead, web, and product UI. The brand speaks the two-tone language of the heavy-machinery world: graphite — the grayish black of the machine fleet — with safety amber, the equipment-yellow of the mine site, as the single working accent.

Color

Charcoal is the ground; graphite is the brand; amber is the signal you use once per surface. Build against semantic roles, not raw values.

Brand

Graphite #303234 is the logo colour and the one flat brand value — on light surfaces only (it reads 1.2:1 on the dark grounds, where the logo inverts to white). Safety amber is the single working accent: primary actions, hazard highlights, stat callouts, the .mm-hazard tape. Amber carries only pit-black ink.

Logo

The Maintain Mining wordmark: the family mark in graphite #303234 on light surfaces, inverting to all-white on dark — MINING letter-spaced to the wordmark's full width. Keep clear space ≥ the cap height on all sides; minimum width 120px.

Files: assets/logo/mark.svg · wordmark-on-dark.svg · wordmark-on-light.svg. Don't stretch or add effects, and never place the graphite mark on a dark ground — use the white version. On imagery (fourth tile) the white wordmark sits over a ~50% charcoal scrim so it never fights the terrain behind it.
